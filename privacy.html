Privacy Policy

Your privacy is extremely important to Hamza Coffee Shop. This document outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when using our Order Ahead service.

1. Information We Collect

When placing an online pickup order or reservation, we collect your name, phone number, and email address strictly to fulfill your order and notify you when your drink is ready.

2. Data Security

We do not store credit card credentials on local storage. All client data is encrypted and handled under modern security best practices.
