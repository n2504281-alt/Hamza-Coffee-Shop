Privacy Policy

Your privacy is extremely important to us. Last updated: January 2026.

Your privacy is extremely important to Hamza Coffee Shop. This document outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when using our Order Ahead service and website.

1. Information We Collect

When placing an online pickup order or reservation, we collect your name, phone number, and email address strictly to fulfill your order and notify you when your drink is ready.

2. Data Security

We do not store credit card credentials on local storage. All client data is encrypted and handled under modern security best practices. We use industry-standard SSL encryption for all data transmissions.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share anonymized, aggregated data with service providers who assist us in operating our website, provided they agree to keep this information confidential.

4. Cookies

Our website uses browser localStorage to save your cart and wishlist for a seamless shopping experience. No tracking cookies are sent to external advertising networks.

5. Contact About Privacy

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@hamzacoffee.com or through our Contact Page.
