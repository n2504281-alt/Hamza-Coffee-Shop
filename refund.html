Refund & Cancellation Policy

We strive for 100% satisfaction with every beverage and pastry served.

At Hamza Coffee Shop, your satisfaction is our top priority. If you're not completely satisfied with your order, we're here to make it right. Please review our policy below.

1. Order Cancellations

Pickup orders can be canceled up to 5 minutes prior to the scheduled pickup time for a full refund or drink replacement. Cancellations made after preparation has begun may be eligible for a store credit at our discretion.

2. Drink Replacement Guarantee

If your beverage temperature or customization is not to your exact liking, inform our barista on site and we will gladly re-brew your cup instantly at zero charge. Our baristas are committed to perfecting your drink every time.

3. Pastry & Food Items

Freshly baked pastries are non-refundable once prepared. However, if a pastry item fails to meet our quality standards, we will gladly replace it with a fresh item or offer store credit for a future visit.

4. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed within 3–5 business days to the original payment method. For cash payments, refunds are issued immediately in store. Please keep your order confirmation for reference.

5. Contact Support

For any refund or cancellation requests, contact us at support@hamzacoffee.com or visit our Contact Page. We respond within 24 hours.
